Questions

How does Receptr actually answer calls?

Receptr connects to your existing phone number via telephony integration. When a call comes in, the AI receptionist picks up, greets the caller by your business name, and handles the conversation — just like a human would.

Can it speak Hindi and English?

Yes. Receptr is built for natural Hindi, English, and Hinglish — the way Indian callers actually speak. It understands code-mixed sentences and responds naturally in the same language pattern.

Can it transfer calls to a human?

Yes. When a caller needs personal attention or raises an urgent issue, Receptr hands the call off to a designated team member or number cleanly, with context already gathered.

Can it handle bookings and common questions?

Yes. It can collect appointment details, confirm bookings, answer FAQs about timing, pricing, location, and services — and escalate when a question is outside its scope.

How long does setup take?

We handle the full onboarding. A typical setup — connecting your number, configuring call flows, and testing — takes two to five business days depending on your business type.

Is it suitable for my type of business?

Receptr works for any business that receives regular inbound calls — clinics, salons, diagnostic centers, coaching institutes, real estate offices, and similar Indian SMBs. If your front desk handles calls, Receptr can help.
